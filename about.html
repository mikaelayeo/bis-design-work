About this site

What this is

A single, browsable home for the design work I generate — wireframes, prototypes, presentations, and component documentation — so it's all clickable in one place instead of scattered HTML files.

Showcase holds finalized, polished pieces meant to be shared with anyone. Studio is my soft-gated working library: drafts, internal decks, analysis, and learnings I keep for reference and promote to Showcase when they're ready.
