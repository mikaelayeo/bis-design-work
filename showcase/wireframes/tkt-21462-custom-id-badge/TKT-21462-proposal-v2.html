A. How It Works Reused patternPDF tooling

Excelsior needs their own branded badge, an optional profile photo, and free placement of the QR code, logo, text and photo. Admins design the badge anywhere, make it a fillable PDF in Adobe Acrobat, and upload it. There is no visual editor (Sachin's approach, Aug 11, about 32 h dev). Badges are still downloaded from each user's profile; that flow doesn't change.

1 Download the sample
A fillable 54 × 86 mm badge with all 15 fields placed, or start from your own artwork.
2 Name the fields
In Acrobat, name each form field exactly as in section F. Text fields for text, image (button) fields for pictures.
3 Upload and check
The page shows "N of M recognized" and a preview with sample data (sections C, E).
4 Use custom template
Confirm once and all badges use the PDF. Switch back any time.
  • NewPage 1 is the front, page 2 the back (optional). Size and orientation come from the PDF page. Images scale to fit and keep their proportions; text uses the font set on the field in the PDF.
  • NewNo photo box means no photo. Leave ProfileImage out and no photo prints — this covers "photo optional".
  • ReusedThe approach follows the Adobe Embedded certificate templates on TRM Edit Course.
In the prototype: open Prototype v2 and go to the Custom PDF template tab.
Confirmed: one PDF template per portal; any field without data, or with a name not in the list, prints blank.
