Rules that apply across every ticket 12

  1. Expected vs assigned. Work is assigned to a person only once they're confirmed on site.Shown on: Site · Requirements · Site · People · App: App: Field · App: App: Site
  2. Signing in means being confirmed at work, by any method Signing into a Job at a Site also signs them into the Site, never the other way round.Shown on: Job · People · Attendance · App: App: worker Field · App: App: Attendance
  3. Owed once. A ritual that a Job and its Site both carry is done once.Shown on: Site · Requirements · Job · Requirements
  4. Missed work carries over marked as the earlier day's; reports count it on the day it was owedShown on: Person page · Site · Submissions · App: App: worker To Do · App: App: Site · Requirements
  5. History is never rewritten. Changes to settings, rituals and crews apply from the next dayShown on: Manage › Site · Manage › Rituals · Settings
  6. Created on the web; moved on the app. Sites, Jobs, rituals and crews are created on the web only.Shown on: Manage › Site · App: App: Site · Requirements · App: App: Site · Crews
  7. One place to manage each object. "Add new" anywhere else links to Manage or reuses the same pop-up, never a second buildShown on: Site · Crews · Manage › Crew · Settings (Sharing)
  8. Training never blocks and is never highlighted: Expired and Expiring only, in grey.Shown on: Site · People · People list · App: App: Site · People
  9. One status vocabulary across the product based on the Training Matrix Summary Report (Oct 13).Shown on: Site · Submissions · Site · Requirements · App: App: Site · Requirements
  10. Tables: page size 25, 50 or 100; more rows load on scroll, and Load more stays. Group by resets when you leave a page; saved filters stay.Shown on: Site · Submissions · Sites list · My preferences
  11. The People list only holds people on a Site, Job or crew. "Open in Users" only shows to someone allowed to see that profileShown on: People list · Person page · Person page as a viewer
  12. Labels from a preset list only. "Group" is never used as a client-facing word.Shown on: Settings (Kinds and labels) · Day one setup
