B. Getting Louder As the Deadline Gets Closer New — being proposed

As a trial gets closer to ending, we suggest the banner's color and wording become more urgent — same banner, just harder to miss the closer you get to the deadline.

⚠ More than 14 days left — standard banner, as shown above
⚠ 3–14 days left — "Your Forms trial ends in 6 days. Contact your account manager now to keep full access."
⚠ Less than 3 days left — "Your Forms trial ends tomorrow. Notify your account manager today to avoid losing admin access."

E. What Happens After the Trial Ends (60-Day Countdown to Deletion) New — nothing like this exists today

This part was never built for TKT-16203. The current plan is: one warning pop-up at day 90, then everything gets permanently deleted 60 days after that with no further notice. We think that's risky — a client could easily miss or forget a single early warning. We'd suggest:

⚠ Suggested reminder (30 days before deletion): "Forms data will be permanently deleted in 30 days. Export your data or notify your account manager to keep access."
  • Send a few reminders across those 60 days (for example, at 30 days and 7 days left), not just one notice right at the start.
  • Add a way for the client to download/export their data before it's deleted, alongside the reminders and inside the Trial Expired pop-up.
  • Ask for a clear, separate confirmation right before the actual deletion happens — not just rely on something the client acknowledged 60 days earlier.
F. Trial Override Switch New
Folders — Module Settings (new settings area; Folders doesn't have one today)
Trial Override Extends the client's 90-day trial period, allowing continued access to the module in Trial Mode until this setting is disabled.
Countdown suspended — trial will not expire until this is turned off.

We suggest adding that status line under the switch, so it's obvious at a glance the countdown is paused — not something you only find out by reading the switch's small-print label.

When this is turned on: the banner drops the end date — "Your Classroom Calendar is in trial mode. Please contact your account manager if you would like to access the full features of this module." The watermark on documents and the Notify button stay as-is.

G. Internal Company List (BIS staff only) Needs a decision

The internal company list that BIS staff use already color-codes each module's status:

Green = paying customer, orange = trialing and within their usage limit, red = trialing and over their usage limit. Worth deciding whether that's in scope for this ticket, or a follow-up.

What We Reused vs. Proposed

  • The banner, the Trial Expired pop-up, and the confirmation pop-up are the same ones already live today wherever the ticket doesn't call for new behavior — this is an update, not a rebuild.
  • The four new banner spots (Rewards, Playbooks, Driver Management, Lone Worker) use the exact same banner design — just new places to put it, once the related admin-page work (TKT-18451) ships.
  • The "getting louder" banner idea reuses the same banner shape with different wording and color — not a new design, just new copy rules.
  • Everything about the 60-day deletion countdown (Section E) is genuinely new — there's no existing version of this to reuse.
