E. What Happens After the Trial Ends (60-Day Countdown to Deletion) ✓ Confirmed — Error banners + Error modal

After the trial ends (day 90), the client has 60 more days before their trial data is permanently deleted. We send reminders as the date approaches, then one final confirmation right before it happens.

1. Reminders during the 60-day window — design system Error banner

Your Forms trial data will be permanently deleted in 30 days. Contact your account manager to keep full access, or to export your data before it's deleted.
Your Forms trial data will be permanently deleted in 7 days. Contact your account manager to keep full access, or to export your data before it's deleted.
Your Forms trial data will be permanently deleted tomorrow. Contact your account manager now to keep full access, or to export your data before it's deleted.

2. Exporting data — through the account manager

There's no self-serve export in BIS — clients can't download the things they build (courses, form templates) on their own. So during this 60-day window, the account manager is the single path for both options: a client contacts them to either make the module full access, or export their data before it's deleted. The banners and the final pop-up above both say this, so there's no self-serve "export" button that promises something the system doesn't do.

3. Final confirmation — design system Error modal

On the last day, one clear pop-up. There's no "cancel deletion" button and no X — the only way to keep the data is through the account manager. The two options are Confirm, or Notify Account Manager:

Data will be deleted today
All data in this module will be permanently deleted today. This cannot be undone. Please confirm, or contact your account manager if you would like to keep access to this module or export your data.
One thing to confirm: in your Section E note you listed three reminders (30, 7, and 1 day), and in your written answer you mentioned two (30 and 7). We've built in all three above — easy to drop the 1-day one if you'd rather keep it to two.
