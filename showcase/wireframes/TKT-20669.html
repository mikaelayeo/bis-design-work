Round 1 — scope alignment, grounded in Luke's POCs

Components Used

  • Companies list + tabs (Section B) — reused live list; new "Onboarding" filter tab and "Closed — didn't proceed" status.
  • Pricing models (Section C) — reused; already configurable in the system, just linked to this screen.
  • DS buttons, warning banner, status chips — standard design-system components.
  • Wizard stepper + module grid + sticky pricing summary (Section E) — rebuilt on BIStrainer tokens, patterned on Luke's client-self-signup.html.

Decisions Confirmed

  • Scope = proposal → payment only; portal creation is manual / out of scope.
  • Two paths, one wizard — sales-assisted (config before send) vs. self sign-up (blank, standard pricing, no options).
  • Pending profiles live in the Companies list, aren't billed, and use a "Closed — didn't proceed" status for drop-offs.
  • Custom pricing → mandatory Accounting approval; standard pricing never needs it.
  • Client reply = accept, or decline with notes; no direct price editing. Steps auto-unlock once configured — no waiting on a person.

Still Open

  • Signatory routing — on sales-assisted, the contract may need to go to a specific designated signer (client's contract contact), not just whoever signs up. Undesigned — needs Luke.
  • Proposal versioning — must retain V1 / V2 / V3 history when a client requests changes, not overwrite.
  • Payment mechanism (Section F) — how payment is actually accepted; Luke to confirm with Sam P / Accounting.
  • Locked-stage editing — once a stage (e.g. signed contract) is locked, there's no edit; it's cancel-and-restart. Needs a defined cancel path.
  • Adjacent tickets? — post-signup Pricing Dashboard (self-enable/trial modules, disable-requires-data-purge, notifies account manager) + first-login pricing confirmation. Likely separate.
