BIS Safety Software's Design System: starter sheet

A small sample of BIS Safety Software's Design System: the core colour tokens and a handful of components. It's a starting point, not the full system. Build any other components you need yourself.

Heads up: the current Assets screens still use older Bootstrap styling (the teal and blue buttons you'll see there). The design system on this page is where our UI is heading. Your redesign doesn't have to match either one exactly, but explain the choices you make.
