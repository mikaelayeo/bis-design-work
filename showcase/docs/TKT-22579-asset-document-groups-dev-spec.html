1 · What the user gets

Display only. The Documents tab shows the groups an admin made on web, on a redesigned section. Mobile does not create, rename, remove or move groups. No drag and drop, no archive, no edit.
  • Documents section: sort, a Grouping toggle, a small Upload button, group cards above ungrouped documents.
  • Every document is a card. Tap the card to open it. No eye icon.
  • One action on a card: the upload icon. No file yet: it uploads. A file already there: it replaces. The existing upload and replace modals and snackbars stay as they are.
  • No upload button on a group. Upload lives on a card and on the section.
  • All three sections (Required, Documents, Archived) use the Accordion component.

Group List Card spec

From Figma node 7017:2808 and the SafeTapp Storybook

  • Width 368. Header fill color/surface/default, radius 8, shadow 0 0 3 rgba(0,0,0,.25), padding 12.
  • Closed: two layers stacked underneath (354 and 362 wide, offset 8 and 4), meaning "more of these". Open: layers gone, 1px border color/stroke/default, children 12 below.
  • Title row: folder icon (10.5×14, gap 6), then label 16/24 Semibold, two lines then an ellipsis. Count pill: fill color/surface/secondary-lightest, radius 80, padding 2/6, number 14/18 Semibold secondary text, chevron 8×5 (turns 180° when open). Counts above 99 show "99+".
  • Controlled component: the screen owns expanded. Open and close take 300 ms. Reduced motion: instant.
  • The screen passes translated strings: expandedAnnouncement, collapsedAnnouncement, showChildrenLabel, hideChildrenLabel, accessibilityLabel.

4 · Behaviour

#Rule
1Documents section order: sort control row, then groups, then a separator, then ungrouped documents. Required and Archived are not grouped.
2The Grouping toggle is hidden when the asset has no groups (a flat list only). When groups exist the toggle shows. Off = one flat list. On = groups. To confirm: which state it opens on (ticket and web: Grouped once a group exists).
2aSection Upload with groups: tap Upload, then the group choice modal, then Select, then the existing source sheet and form. With no groups the modal is skipped. The card's upload icon never shows the modal: replace keeps the group, and uploading to a Not Uploaded document keeps its group.
3Groups are collapsed by default. Tap the header to open or close.
4Card order in the flat list, inside a group and in the ungrouped list: 1. Expired, 2. Not Uploaded, 3. Uploaded. Sort applies inside each of these three bands.
5Sort options: Name, Last Uploaded Date. Sort applies to the groups (their order) and to the cards inside each status band.
6The group card shows the total count, and "Expired: n" when n > 0.
7Expired documents stay visible while their group is collapsed. See flag 11.
8Tapping a card opens the document in the existing viewer (download and share stay there). The eye icon is removed.
9Upload icon on a card: Not Uploaded document → upload. Uploaded or Expired document → replace. Replace keeps the document's group.
10Read-only or inactive asset: groups show and open, but there is no Upload button and no upload icon on cards.
11Documents with no group sit below the groups. A document belongs to one group at most.

States to design or confirm: no groups, no documents, toggle off with groups present, read-only asset, long group name (two lines, then an ellipsis), 99+ count, offline, tablet.

7 · Flags

Design vs. design system

  1. Accordion body background and padding differ between the design (#f8f8f8, 24 bottom, 16 gap) and the code (color/surface/default, 14 bottom).
  2. File-type icons (docx, PDF) are not in the library.
  3. Status chips beyond Expired are not confirmed. Only Expired and the forms chips were found.
  4. The document card is a List Card without the left bar and with an icon button in the ⋮ slot. Confirm this configuration exists.
  5. In the design frames, List Card, Group List Card, M-Button and the card icons are detached frames, not instances. Relink them so the specs follow the components.
  6. The tap target on the card's upload icon is 28px.

Design vs. ticket

  1. Group choice on upload: resolved. A select modal appears when the section Upload is tapped on an asset with groups. The ticket should name "General" as the no-group option.
  2. Grouping default: the toggle is hidden with no groups. Confirm the state it opens on once groups exist. The ticket says All until the first group exists; web switches to Grouped at that point.
  3. Sorting is not in the ticket.
  4. Required and Archived are "unchanged" in the ticket's acceptance criteria. The design restyles both (below).
  5. Expired visible under a collapsed group. The design has the footer "Expired: n". In node 7020:7425 an expired card sits below the collapsed Training group with nothing to say whether it belongs to it.
  6. "Last uploaded" on the group card: removed from the design.
  7. Not yet designed: the grouping-off (flat) state, a group with no documents, the read-only state.
  8. Typo in the copy: "Pressure Vessel ceritifcate.pdf".

Required and Archived: updated look (open for devs to choose)

  • Required, updated: cards with chips (Denied, Not Uploaded, Approved) and an upload icon. The Compliance bar, the settings gear and the eye icon are gone. Confirm those removals are intended and that Approved and Denied are existing statuses.
  • Archived, updated: cards with an "Uploaded" chip and expiry. The eye icon is replaced by tap to open.
  • Option A: Accordion shell only, old content stays. Option B: shell and cards.
  • Recommendation: B, in this ticket if there is room. Otherwise a follow-up ticket straight after. Either way the ticket text changes, because it says these sections are unchanged.

8 · Out of scope

Create, rename or remove a group. Move a document to a group. Drag and drop. Archive. Edit. Bulk move. Group-level upload. Tablet layout (not designed).

9 · Backend questions

From the code graph. A floor, not proof.
  • Web returns the group with the document list: ajaxequipment.getEquipmentDocs has a groupName field. The mobile API getEquipmentDocs (bisapi) was found without groups. It needs to return the group (name, and an id if there is one). Read-only, so no write endpoints are needed.
  • Last uploaded date per document, and a way to tell Not Uploaded from Uploaded. Is a Not Uploaded placeholder a document record in the Documents section?
  • Sorting: web sorts per group. Is sorting done on the page after loading or by the server? Does the mobile list come back whole, or paged?
  • Label keys: web needs 18 new keys, and the ticket does not list them. Mobile needs display strings only: section labels, Grouping, "Show documents in their groups", sort options, status chips, "Expired", the group choice modal, and the screen reader announcements.
