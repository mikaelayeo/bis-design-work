Course Overview

An OSHA-Compliant Training Class Online for defensive driving. This course satisfies OSHA's requirements for the classroom portion of operator safety training. Quiz questions along the way prepare you for the final written exam.

Defensive driving is the practice of anticipating hazards, controlling speed and space, and making decisions that prevent collisions before they happen, regardless of what other road users do. It matters for everyone behind the wheel on company time, from commercial and fleet drivers to service technicians and everyday operators running errands in a light vehicle. Strong defensive driving habits cut collision rates, reduce vehicle and insurance costs, and keep your people and the public safe on every trip.

Learners complete the course at their own pace and can start, stop, and resume at any time. Progress is saved automatically, and all content is delivered on the BIS Learning Platform with team management, certificate tracking, and compliance reporting built in.

Course Topics

This online Defensive Driving (OSHA) course covers the following topics:

  • Introduction
  • Care of Your Vehicle
  • Laws / Controls
  • Conditions
  • Preparedness
  • Conclusion

Who Should Take This Course

This course is designed for anyone who drives on company time, including:

  • Commercial and fleet drivers
  • Service technicians and mobile workers
  • Sales and field staff who drive between sites
  • Any employee operating a light vehicle for work

What You'll Learn

  • Hazard perception and anticipating other road users
  • Controlling speed, space, and safe following distance
  • Collision-avoidance decision making under pressure
  • Adjusting to road, weather, and traffic conditions
