Defensive driving is the practice of anticipating hazards, controlling speed and space, and making decisions that prevent collisions before they happen, regardless of what other road users do. It matters for everyone behind the wheel on company time, from commercial and fleet drivers to service technicians and everyday operators running errands in a light vehicle. Strong defensive driving habits cut collision rates, reduce vehicle and insurance costs, and keep your people and the public safe on every trip.

About defensive driving training

What is defensive driving?

A set of skills for anticipating hazards and avoiding collisions before they happen — controlling speed, space and attention regardless of what other road users do.

Who needs defensive driving training?

Anyone driving on company time: commercial and fleet drivers, service technicians, and everyday operators in light vehicles. Many insurers and OHS programs expect current certification.

How to choose the right course

Start with your vehicle or role, then narrow by conditions (winter, adverse weather) or a required standard (e.g. OSHA). Each course lists what it covers and where its certificate is valid.

Course availability, pricing and certificates

All courses are 100% online, self-paced, and include a certificate of completion. Volume pricing applies for teams — see each course for details.
