Defensive Driving 101

Defensive driving is the practice of anticipating hazards, controlling speed and space, and making decisions that prevent collisions before they happen, regardless of what other road users do. It matters for everyone behind the wheel on company time, from commercial and fleet drivers to service technicians and everyday operators running errands in a light vehicle.
