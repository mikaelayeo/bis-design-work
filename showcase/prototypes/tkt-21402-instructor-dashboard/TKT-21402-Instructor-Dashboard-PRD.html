1Scope

RailPros instructors need to see, during a live classroom exam, which questions the class is getting wrong, which answers people chose and who chose them. Then they can go over a question while people are still taking the exam. This ticket gives the existing Instructor Dashboard (Classroom Calendar → Bookings → Dashboard) a cleaner look and adds one new feature: Results.

Scope rule (Dan, Nov 2): the new look plus the Results list only. Anything today's page doesn't already do is out of this ticket, so it can ship in a couple of weeks. The rest goes to a later ticket, once we have feedback from the clients using the dashboard.

In scope

  • New look for the whole page: header, three panels, course header, Preview, Documents.
  • Participant list with profile pictures and a short status per person.
  • Documents with a coloured icon per type, in grid or list view (as today).
  • Preview and Present, in two screen setups: Extended (laptop plus projector) and Single (one touch screen).
  • Progress window, replacing today's Summary.
  • Results window (new): Most Challenging Questions, Students who need help, and results per course.
  • Manual refresh, as today.

Moved to a later ticket ✕ Dropped

  • Overview cards in the left panel (Most challenging question, Students who need help, Finished).
  • Search and sort for participants and documents.
  • Assigning straight from a document card.
  • Results updating on its own every 10 seconds, and the "Update order" prompt.
  • Clicking a name in Results to open Progress.
  • Sort options inside Progress and Results.
  • The "added after courses were assigned" note and per-person Assign (courses already reach everyone).

Out of scope

  • The separate "Course Overview" tab from the original July request (replaced by this one-dashboard design).
  • Assigning PDFs, videos or photos. Only online courses and quizzes can be assigned.
  • PowerPoint conversion or any file-handling changes.
  • Opening the second window on the projector automatically.
  • Grading or messaging tools.

Goals and success measures

  • An instructor can find the most-missed question, and who missed it, in two clicks from the dashboard.
  • An instructor can see who needs help without leaving the classroom session.
  • Ships within RailPros' timeline (about two weeks from Nov 2) with no new back-end features beyond Results.
  • Follow-up: feedback from Astec, RailPros and one other client using the dashboard decides the later ticket.

Course headerCourse name, instructor, start time, number of participants, screen setup (gear), Discussion Board with unread count.

Results windowLeft list: Overview (Most Challenging Questions, Students who need help) and Online courses. Right: the chosen view. Refresh at the top, privacy note at the bottom.
PresentExtended: a second window for the projector. Single: full screen on the same screen, with a Documents side list and Exit presenting.

B-2Click the tab on the edge of a side panelThe panel hides; click again to show it. Same as today.
B-3Open on a tablet or phoneSide panels open as drawers, one at a time.

IDScenarioExpected
B-12Documents panelIn the order the admin set. Grid or list view. No search or sort.

B-15A .pptx documentWarning icon; Preview explains it can't be shown; Present is disabled for it.
B-16Press Present, Extended screenA second window opens for the projector, showing the current document. Preview shows "On the second screen" and Stop.
B-17Close the second windowPresenting stops on the dashboard.
B-18Press Present, Single screenThe document fills the screen. Documents opens a side list to switch; Exit presenting returns to the dashboard.
B-19Click Assign, pick courses, choose peopleOnly online courses and quizzes can be picked. Assign to everyone or chosen people. People already assigned don't get a second copy.
B-20Document cardsNo assign icon on the card. Assigning is only from the Assign button.

IDScenarioExpected
B-21First visitExtended screen is on. Badge in the Preview bar says so.

5.5 Progress

IDScenarioExpected

B-25Many coursesName and Status (and Wrong answers) stay fixed; course columns scroll sideways.

5.6 Results

IDScenarioExpected
B-29Open ResultsOpens on Most Challenging Questions.
B-30Most Challenging QuestionsEvery question with at least one wrong answer, across all assigned courses, most wrong answers first. All shown open.
B-31Each questionCourse, correct answer, "<n> of <answered> chose a wrong answer", then each wrong option chosen with "<n> chose this" and the names. Options nobody chose aren't listed.
B-32CountingCounts use the people who have answered that question so far, not the whole class.
B-33Two wrong options tieBoth shown; order within a tie is stable.
B-34No wrong answers yetMessage that everyone chose the correct answer so far.
B-35Students who need helpOne card per person with 3 or more wrong answers across all courses, most first. "See wrong questions" lists each question, the option they chose and the correct answer.
B-36Nobody has 3 or more wrong answersMessage that nobody needs help yet.
B-37Pick a courseThat course's questions, most wrong answers first; the top one starts open. Shows "<n> of <total> started".
B-38Course shuffles its questionsNote that results show as people answer.
B-39Present a questionThe question shows on the presenting screen with answer counts only, never names.
B-40Click a name in ResultsNothing happens. Names are labels only.
B-41Single screenCounts first. Show names / Show students reveals names for that item; Hide names hides them; all names hide again when Results closes.

5.7 Refresh

IDScenarioExpected
B-42Results or Progress is openShows "updated <time> ago". Nothing changes on its own.
B-43Press refreshNew answers load and the lists re-sort.
