The live Form Builder already has two signature field types. External Party support needs to slot into whichever one (or both) this ticket covers — flagged as an open question in the sidebar.

"Allows the respondent to add their signature to the form." One signer, filled in while completing the form itself. External Party is just one more plain checkbox alongside the existing ones — no special box or highlight — that reveals an email field underneath when checked.

Route 2 — Multiple Signatures Existing type

"Allows multiple respondents (e.g. Evaluator 1, Evaluator 2, Manager) to sign off on a completed form. This is much closer to how an external party would actually be looped in.
