A. The Flow at a Glance New workflow

A form is set up once with an External Party signature. From then on, any BIS user who fills that form out chooses who signs it by typing an email at submission time. That person gets a link, confirms who they are, reviews the form as a PDF, and signs — and their signature lands back in the form record.
