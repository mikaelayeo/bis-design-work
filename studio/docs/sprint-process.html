Getting Ahead

How do we make sure tickets are fully ready before sprint planning starts — so QA can prep test cases, devs can review, and planning day is actually just planning?

What's breaking right now

Tickets arrive at sprint planning
still half-baked.

🔍

Designs are still being walked through in the planning meeting — not committed to.

⏳

Devs can't estimate without final designs. QA can't write test cases until dev starts.

↩️

Discovery questions surface mid-sprint, pulling PM and Designer back in after sprint has started.

Meeting goal

Leave with three things agreed.

  • 01 A shared definition of what "ready" means — what a ticket must have before it can enter a sprint.
  • 02 A lead time agreement — how many weeks ahead PM needs to flag candidates, and when design needs to be finalized.
  • 03 One thing to try in the June sprint — a concrete pilot, not a policy.

Readiness checklist

A ticket is ready when it has all of these.

This is what the ticket must contain before it gets committed in sprint planning. If anything is missing, it goes back — it doesn't enter the sprint.

✓
Clear problem statement What is the user trying to do? Why is the current experience broken?
✓
Acceptance criteria written Testable conditions. QA can write test cases from these without asking anyone.
✓
Designs finalized (Figma link in ticket) Design is done — not "in progress" or "almost there." Dev can build from it as-is.
✓
No open questions Any discovery or investigation is complete. Scope is agreed. No "let's figure it out in dev."
Size estimate (S / M / L / XL) Dev lead has given a rough estimate. Helps PM prioritize what fits in the sprint.

One thing to try

A June pilot, not a policy.

We're not overhauling the whole process. We pick one thing to try in June, see if it helps, and decide from there.

Proposed pilot — June sprint

A single grooming sync between PM and Designer, two weeks before sprint planning. That's it.

  • Anita flags candidate tickets by May 12
  • Mika and Anita meet for 30 min on May 12 to agree on design priority
  • Mika has designs and AC finalized by May 19
  • Dev lead reviews and estimates by May 26
  • June 2 planning: we commit only to ready tickets
  • After the sprint: we check — did this reduce planning meeting time? Did dev have fewer questions mid-sprint?
