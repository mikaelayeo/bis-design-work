The story in six lines

Top-line takeaways

01
The app is actively blocking their operations.
Workers and admins are visibly distressed. Some have normalized uninstalling and reinstalling daily just to keep it usable. One worker hit 27 crashes in a single morning and switched to paper.
02
Auto-download of 314 assets to all 850 devices is the root cause.
The dev confirmed this on-site. After turning it off mid-visit, a device dropped from ~300 downloads to 14 and "ran a lot smoother."
03
Tech-savvy users are already abandoning the app for the web browser.
"We don't have near the problems on the browser." That's the early-warning sign — the most capable users are routing around us.
04
Our "download-first" data philosophy is the architectural problem.
It needs to flip to cloud-first with opt-in offline — reserve local storage for the few cases (like 28-day remote sites) that actually need it.
05
Even when the app works, the workflows fight the user.
Bulletin sign-off is a 5-step trip. Submitting forms kicks admins to the home screen. Reporting numbers can't be trusted. They've been running a parallel Excel dashboard for 4 years because of it.
06
This is a category-wide gap, not just Park Paving.
Floyd's reporting wishlist — FLRA trends, incidents, WCBs, lost-time, bulletin read rates — is what every HSE team needs.
● Critical Operations are blocked.

What's breaking the app today

01
Auto-download of all assets crashes/lags the app to unusability
314 assets × 850 users. UI not clickable. 3–5 crashes/week typical, 27 in one morning peak.
DirectionLoop in CS and CE. Continue building offline-management features — message modal, auto-remove after X days of no usage. Warning when a user is about to download a lot of data (e.g. "more than XXX assets will cause problems on your device").
02
Duplicate form submissions from lag and crashes
One worker had 9 duplicates of a single FLHA. Admins can't close or reopen them.
DirectionOnly save as in-progress when the user has actually taken an action — empty forms get a snackbar and are discarded. Let users delete duplicates, with multi-select remove.
03
Submit-button multi-tap sends duplicates
Closing duplicates kicks the user back to the home screen and forces full re-navigation. Demonstrated live.
DirectionAsset redesign.
04
Job number input drops characters mid-typing
Recurring bug, triggered when typing during background download. Likely affects other fields too.
DirectionFile as a bug case — check whether attendance fields and other inputs have the same issue.
05
Tablets lock up and force re-login mid-form
Reproduced across multiple devices (T21, 92s, B7, 16108). Likely connected to auto-download pressure.
● High Workflows don't match reality.

Where the design fights the user

07
"Download first, online later" — every tap downloads, no opt-out
Every interaction in the app implicitly downloads data, with no way to opt out. This will keep degrading performance long-term.
DirectionAdd an explicit "make available offline" toggle. Tapping data should not auto-download it. If an admin sets an asset as available offline, the user toggle is disabled. Look across other industries we serve to see how they handle this — think YouTube Music's recent files.
08
Historical data bulks up the database with old jobs/FLRAs
Annual contracts mean prior-year data is rarely referenced. We need a better auto-archive / historical system.
DirectionSame UX pattern as the offline feature — surface what's old enough to archive, let users (or admins) decide.
09
Bulletin sign-off is multi-step when it should be one tap
Open → read → back → sign → submit. User's phrase: a "digital fingerprint" the moment they open it.
10
FLRA email sends on end-of-day, not on morning fill-out
Latecomers can't review or sign remotely. Compliance is the reading and signing, not the physical presence.
DirectionAttendance feature.
11
No "form already submitted today" detection
System isn't aware of daily cadence. Smart dedupe + auto-delete extras + a "what you did today" history page would close the gap.
12
Pending-forms triage flow is buried and ungrouped
Asset → on-demand form → pending → warning modal. Admins triage blind, with no date grouping.
● Medium Reporting trust is broken.

Field notes

What I saw on the floor

  • Users kept hitting "Submit" early just to see which fields were required — required-field visibility is failing upstream.
  • A worker with an older Android device (likely Samsung) struggled the most — performance baseline matters, our floor isn't where we think it is.
  • Young workers were handing tablets over to be signed without anyone reading the assessment — digitization is undermining the safety value itself, which is a product-design problem, not just a UX one.
Closing framing

What this means for us

"It should have never reached this point where their operations are halted due to our services."

The trust dynamic is the underlying story: customers were so used to working around the product that the workarounds looked normal. Fixing the auto-download is the unlock — but it doesn't restore trust on its own. The workflow fixes — bulletin one-tap, dedupe logic, dashboard parity — are what prove we listened.
