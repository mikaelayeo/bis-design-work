The takeaway
Across all tickets reviewed, active design work represents roughly 10–15% of total calendar time. The remaining 85–90% is waiting: waiting for scope clarity, waiting for review, waiting for stakeholder alignment, waiting for prioritization, and waiting for development pickup. The highest-leverage improvements target the waiting, not the designing — specifically, tighter review loops, a definition of ready/done, and a requirement freeze before development starts.
