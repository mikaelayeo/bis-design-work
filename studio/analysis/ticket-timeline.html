The Core Insight
Active design work is only 10–15% of total calendar time. The remaining 85–90% is organizational overhead: waiting on stakeholder feedback, iterating through revision loops, attending alignment meetings, navigating scope changes, and supporting development. The bottleneck isn't design capacity — it's the system around design.
