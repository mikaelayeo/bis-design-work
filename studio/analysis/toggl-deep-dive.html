🔴 The 14290 Effect
AI Course Builder consumed 224.9 hours — 12% of Year 1 alone. Pat's 1-on-1 estimated "~100 hours." The actual was 2.25× that estimate. This ticket had a 20+ month lifecycle spanning both years, driven by cascading feedback loops (Erica's 100-point review, multiple proposal meetings, handoff to Techversant, and ongoing dev support). It represents the archetype of what happens when scope isn't bounded at the organizational level.
⚠️ #17024 — Blocked for 10 Months
eCommerce Redesign accumulated 42.7 hours of design work and then never entered a dev sprint. 40+ hours of professional effort sitting on a shelf — a direct cost of having no prioritized design roadmap.

🔴 84.5 Hours Looking for Work
This is the most damning number in the dataset. 84.5 hours — over two full work weeks per year — spent searching for the next thing to do. This is a direct, measurable artifact of operating without a design roadmap or product management. It represents not just wasted time, but lost flow state and eroded morale.
07 — Key Insights
What the numbers prove
1. The 14290 Effect
One ticket consumed 12% of Year 1. Estimated at ~100 hrs, actual was 224.9. Estimation without a design lens will always undershoot complex creative work.
2. Quick-Touch Treadmill
63% of tickets are under 2 hours. The workflow is overwhelmingly reactive — punctuated by rare marathon projects that nobody plans for.
3. Executor → Strategist
The shift from 49% tickets to 45% initiatives is visible in the numbers, not just a feeling. This is a measurable career evolution.
4. The Roadmap Void
84 hours "looking for next ticket" — a direct artifact of no roadmap, no PM. Two weeks per year evaporating.
5. Design System = Invisible
113 hours invested in design systems — the single largest initiative — yet largely invisible to leadership. This needs a visibility strategy.
6. Overhead Efficiency
Overhead dropped from 25.5% → 16.2%. That's 170 fewer hours spent on non-productive activities in Year 2. You got sharper.
7. Zombie Drag
33 tickets spanning both years — work that never closes. Each one carries invisible context-switching costs that compound weekly.
8. Collaboration is Growing
Aymeric time grew 6× (7.7 → 46.6 hrs). Mahi onboarded at 14.4 hrs. The helping-people category tripled. You're becoming a force multiplier.
